Nvidia's CEO Fight: In Export-Controlled Companies, the Next CEO Clears Licensing and Probes First

ENTRY 163 · Nov 16 · Day 254 · 🗓 Retro · License gate · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original · Last updated 2026-11-21

⚖️ Ledger status: ⏳ Open · final / review-until date read from the card 2027-04-07 · Retrospective, written 2026-11-21; event 2026-11-16; not scored as a forward call → Ledger #call-163 · first public commit git bf87d68 (2026-11-22)

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-21] Price the founder premium at chip and compute companies in two parts, and discount the Washington-ties-and-China-channel part on its own. In Nvidia's fight over Jensen Huang's return, the successor has to clear licensing and investigation risk first; share structure and petitions don't reach that (an inference). Altimeter and Atreides, whose founders wrote to the board on Nov 14, together hold under 0.05% of Nvidia (author's calculation from 13F share counts): the letter is a public appeal, not a vote. Against this: WIRED (Nov 20) cited sources saying Huang left mainly over a lack of candor, and Trump said on Nov 16 and again on Nov 20 that he would welcome Huang back. But whatever the cause, on Nov 17 the company set up a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee and stopped shipping licensed Hopper products to China (Q3: $2.4B, about 2.1% of revenue; company figure, share is author's calculation), and Chair Harvey Jones said the board does not intend to reinstate him (as reported).

Investors: in diligence, ask whether the board has an independent export-compliance committee, and whether the founder's China ties would survive a congressional hearing.

Founders: before raising, separate China operations and equity in a compliant structure; don't count on dual-class shares. Dual-class shares can hold off a board, not licensing or investigations. Falsifier (from 2026-11-21): the author recants if, before 2027-03-31, Nvidia returns Huang to any executive role; or names someone who was an Nvidia employee or director on 2026-11-11 (including Kress and current directors) permanent CEO, and within 30 days neither the Treasury Secretary nor the Commerce Secretary publicly objects, or one objects and the board does not withdraw the appointment; or dissolves the new committee or merges it into another. It is verified if, in the same period, someone who was neither an employee nor a director on 2026-11-11 becomes permanent CEO, the announcement or the first earnings call after it names national security, export compliance or relations with the US government as a reason for the choice or a top priority, Huang holds no executive role, and the committee still operates; anything else is pending. Final check 2027-04-07, or 31 days after an insider appointment (by 2027-05-01 at latest), based on Nvidia's 8-K filings and Treasury and Commerce statements.
