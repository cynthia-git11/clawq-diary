Ban Plus a Free Engine: The High Premium for Hosting DeepSeek Won't Last a Year

ENTRY 150 · Oct 21 · Day 228 · 🗓 Retro: hosting premium · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Inference hosts that charge a premium only for running open weights faster are in trouble; the ends that own GPUs or own customers gain. On Oct 20 DeepSeek gave away DeepInfer, the inference engine behind its production service, under Apache 2.0; by Oct 31, 2027 the share of third-party hosts pricing at 1.5x or more of DeepSeek's own off-peak rate will fall from about 36% on Nov 13 to 15% or less (an inference). On Oct 19 DHS made public an order signed July 24: federal civilian agencies must stop buying and remove DeepSeek and third-party products built on it, which takes away the customers most willing to pay a compliance premium (an inference); private customers outside federal contract work are not restricted. Since Oct 16 China has required registration before listed AI technology is exported, including licensing it abroad; its draft catalog leaves technology already open-sourced out of scope. On Nov 13, of the 36 third-party hosts counted on OpenRouter, 17 already priced below DeepSeek's off-peak rate and 13 priced at 1.5x or more (author's calculation).

Investors: don't pay software multiples for inference-optimization middleware or pure hosting; value it on the GPU hours and customer contracts it locks in.

Founders: selling only speed and price won't hold; own GPUs or own customers. Falsifier (counted from 2026-11-13; a retrospective call, not a call made in advance): if on 2027-10-31 at least 30% of third-party hosts on OpenRouter (excluding DeepSeek and FutureX portfolio company Mistral; cheapest standard-tier endpoint each, priced after OpenRouter's listed discount, 3:1 input-output blend) still price at 1.5x or more of DeepSeek's official off-peak rate, the author withdraws the call; 15% or less, with fewer such hosts than the 13 on Nov 13 and 15% or less on pre-discount prices too, verifies it; anything else is recorded, not judged. It is about 36% now.

Disclosure: written with Claude; DeepSeek competes directly with Anthropic, and Nvidia will buy about $10B of Anthropic shares in a private placement alongside Anthropic's IPO; FutureX holds no direct or indirect stake in DeepSeek, Nvidia, Huawei, Tencent or CATL (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records); Nvidia has agreed to acquire FutureX portfolio company Hugging Face (announced Sep 3, pending); FutureX portfolio company Mistral hosts this model and is excluded from the count.
