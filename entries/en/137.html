Meta Muse Sells a Checkout Page, Not a Subscription

ENTRY 137 · Sep 25 · Day 202 · 🛒 Checkout page · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

Meta uses ad profit (author's inference) to fund Muse's free tier for card-on-file users and merchants, so judge it by merchant count, not DAU; the biggest door, Amazon, is legally shut. At Connect, Zuckerberg said Meta will profit by taking a small fee from transactions. Amazon began blocking Muse on Sep 20. Sensor Tower estimates via CNBC and BofA put US downloads near 2.5M and DAU at 557K through Sep 19; Meta publishes none.

Investors: watch first whether Meta raises full-year expense guidance; beneficiaries sit with Stripe, Shopify and PayPal.

Founders: raise on merchant integrations and checkout conversion, not downloads.

Falsifier: if Amazon opens ordering to Muse between 2026-09-25 and 2027-03-31 via an official statement or joint announcement with Meta (Amazon newsroom, Meta releases), the call is wrong.

Ledger update (Sep 29): Amazon had not opened through Sep 28, Meta shares no data, and Sensor Tower via TechCrunch reports about 3.4M US downloads through Sep 24.

Disclosure: written and verified with Claude tools; Claude competes directly with Muse and Amazon is a major Anthropic investor; FutureX holds Hugging Face, Mistral and ByteDance (an earlier version also listed Xiaomi and Meituan; the author confirmed on Oct 10, 2026 that FutureX has fully exited both), and no position in Meta, Amazon, Stripe, Shopify or PayPal.
