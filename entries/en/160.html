Anthropic's 20% Price Cut: When Buyers Cap Dollars, Cheaper Tokens Don't Add Revenue

ENTRY 160 · Nov 10 · Day 248 · 🗓 Retro · Capped · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] For investors in Anthropic's IPO: when buyers cap AI spend in dollars, a price cut does not turn into revenue. Fable 5.2, released Nov 9, cut per-token prices 20%. A customer at its cap buys 25% more tokens with the same money (author's calculation); if Anthropic keeps the same share of that cap, revenue is flat and cost rises (an inference). Growth from the cut can only come from uncapped customers or from share taken from rivals. In October Apple cut some engineers' daily AI allowance from $300 to $200 (Bloomberg, Nov 4, secondhand); few used the full amount. The cost of serving customers was 14.3% of Anthropic's Q3 revenue (author's calculation from the Oct 26 S-1 and Nov 2 S-1/A); after a per-token cut it should rise first, capped or not (an inference). X post counts for the window are unavailable; Hacker News carried the heat.

Investors: before the listing, ask for the share of uncapped revenue, which the S-1 does not break out; in the first annual report and the Q1 2027 report, check whether quarter-over-quarter revenue growth gets back to Q3's 46.7% (author's calculation); ask app companies for model cost as a share of revenue and whether their customers set daily budgets.

Founders: don't resell tokens at a markup; price on outcomes, so price cuts land in your own margin (an inference). Falsifier (from 2026-11-13): if Q4 2026 or Q1 2027 revenue growth is 46.7% or more (Anthropic's FY2026 10-K and Q1 2027 10-Q, final check 2027-05-31), the main call is withdrawn and counted wrong; if both quarters stay below, it is only not falsified. If Q4 serving cost is 14.34% of revenue or lower (FY2026 10-K, final check 2027-04-15), the 'rises first' inference is withdrawn; above 14.8% verifies it; between the two it is 'direction right, size short', not a hit; no 10-K or no functional breakdown means the source is missing. If Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ or The Information report by 2027-05-13 that Apple raised those engineers' daily allowance above $200 or dropped it, the premise is withdrawn; a new report after 2026-11-13 (not a re-citation of the Nov 4 story) that it stayed at $200 or fell to $100 verifies it; neither means not falsified. Until then, pending.
