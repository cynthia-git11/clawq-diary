Ballots Mostly Stalled Planned Gigawatts: Price Data Centers by Megawatt Status

ENTRY 158 · Nov 6 · Day 244 · 🗓 Retro · MW status · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Whoever leans on planned GW in pause states for valuation deserves a discount, hyperscalers included; whoever holds energized MW deserves a premium. The vote mainly stalled those unpermitted planned GW; AI demand, energized MW and most under-construction MW were not stalled. Cleanview (Nov 6, secondhand): about 41% of planned US capacity sits in states whose governor or governor-elect backs a pause. On Nov 4 the market split by company type, Microsoft up 6.7% and Core Scientific down 10.4% (neither reported earnings that day); by Nov 12 both were back within 1% of their Nov 3 closes (author's calculation). CoreWeave's Q3 report (as of Sep 30, reported Nov 10): backlog of about $132.5B, with energized power only about 37% of contracted power (company figures, author's calculation).

Investors: split data center names into three columns; pay a lease-cash-flow premium on energized and under-construction MW, price planned MW as an option, and discount pause-state planned MW once more; in diligence, ask first how much planned GW sits in pause states.

Founders: build tools that make data centers good neighbors: interruptible load, storage, community revenue sharing, siting compliance. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13, final check 2027-03-31): the call is wrong if two or more of Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon report revenue backlog at 2026-12-31 below the 2026-09-30 level, two or more hyperscalers cut 2027 capex for reasons other than capacity, permitting or delivery delays, or OpenAI confirms on the record a cut to its global 2027 compute target for demand or funding reasons (relocations forced by state permitting excluded); if, after 2026-11-13, any state orders already-started data center projects to stop work (Texas's existing freeze does not count), or no state is running a statewide approval pause on 2027-03-31; if CBRE's H2 2026 report shows primary-market 10MW+ asking rents rising less than H1's +6.7% while vacancy rises above 1.4%; or if a pause state exempts large projects that meet bill-paying or community-benefit conditions while other developers stay paused. None of these, with H2 rents up at least 6.7% or vacancy below 1.4%, verifies it.

Disclosure: written with Claude; Microsoft and Nvidia invested in Anthropic and, with Google, are among the subscribers to its IPO concurrent private placement; Amazon and Google are Anthropic investors and cloud suppliers; CoreWeave and Hut 8 have compute and data center deals with Anthropic; OpenAI competes with it; and Anthropic, now on its IPO roadshow, is a large buyer of data center capacity. FutureX holds no direct or indirect stake in OpenAI, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, CoreWeave, IREN, Hut 8, Core Scientific or Applied Digital (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records). Nvidia has announced a deal to acquire FutureX-held Hugging Face (pending close). FutureX-held Mistral is a CoreWeave customer and also sells compute from its own European data centers; Microsoft agreed in July 2026 to rent capacity from them; Nvidia and Microsoft have investment or partnership ties with Mistral.
