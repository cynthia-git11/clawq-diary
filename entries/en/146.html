Microsoft's Decision-1 Didn't Undercut Jev; Big Tech Sells the Cage

ENTRY 146 · Oct 13 · Day 220 · 🗓 Retro · The cage · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Big Tech did not cut its own list prices for decision models. My inference is that it sells the cage, not the unit price, and that independent decision APIs will be squeezed on direct-sales acquisition and renewals by cloud vendors' own catalogs and by self-hosting, which will reach their next-round valuation multiples in 6–18 months. On Oct 9 Microsoft priced Decision-1 at $0.042 per million input tokens, output free, the same as Jev; OpenAI (from Oct 6) and Google (Nov 11) charge about 2.4 times that (author's calculation). Open-weight hosting was already below Jev in early October; the three companies' list prices had not followed by Nov 13. The cage is what Nadella's Oct 10 essay puts outside the model: permissions, emergency brake, audit. On Nov 9 Amazon said its ticketing system had swapped Sonnet 4.6 for a self-hosted model and cut cost 98% (company claim, one case, still on AWS). TypeSafe was valued at $7.5B on Oct 9 with no revenue officially disclosed as of Nov 13. Counter-reading: on the Vercel gateway this diary uses as a meter, Jev's combined request share rose from a 25.9% to a 32.6% seven-day average between Oct 7–13 and Nov 6–12 (author's calculation), so the squeeze is limited to direct sales and renewals (an inference).

Investors: price decision and guardrail companies on distribution outside cloud vendors, multi-cloud neutrality and self-hosting migration tools, not adoption counts.

Founders: don't compete on base model or unit price; make the base-swap switch the product. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13): by 2027-03-31, Microsoft, Alphabet, OpenAI or Amazon cuts the list price of its own decision model below its Nov 13 level (Microsoft $0.042, OpenAI and Google $0.10 per million input tokens), launches a paid one below $0.042, or makes a paid tier free (rate-limited prototype tiers, promotions of 90 days or less, open weights, third-party hosts, cached-input and per-question bundles, volume or private discounts excluded): any of these makes the price call wrong; none of that by then verifies it. By 2027-11-13, TypeSafe officially announces a new round or sale at $7.5B or more, or an independent decision-model company officially discloses annualized revenue of $100M or more for a period ending after Nov 13: either makes the squeeze call wrong; an official TypeSafe round or sale below $7.5B verifies it, and neither by the deadline leaves it undecided.

Disclosure: this diary is written with Claude (Anthropic) tools; Microsoft, Amazon and Alphabet invest in or supply compute to Anthropic and also compete with it through their clouds, OpenAI competes with it, the model Amazon replaced in the case cited here is Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Anthropic has accused Alibaba of distilling Claude to train Qwen. FutureX holds no direct or indirect stake in Microsoft, TypeSafe, OpenAI, Alphabet, Amazon, Alibaba or Anthropic (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records); FutureX holds Hugging Face, which benefits from the self-hosting case, so discount that part.
