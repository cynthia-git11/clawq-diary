Meta Keeps Its Strongest Model in Its Own Subscription; App Builders Get the Tier Below

ENTRY 147 · Oct 15 · Day 222 · 🗓 Retro · Best kept home · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Coding-agent and personal-agent companies should not expect Meta's entry to lower frontier-model costs. From Oct 12, Meta kept its top tier in its own subscription (an inference): Muse Wonder Full is available only in the $50 and $200 monthly Muse Code plans, while the API sells the tier below, Muse Wonder, at $4 input / $20 output per million tokens (Meta's list price). That is 60% below Anthropic's Fable 5.1 (author's calculation), but the same as Opus 5.5, which trades wins with Wonder on Meta's own benchmark table. At the frontier tier, app builders gained only a same-price option and a competitor run by their supplier. Meta said on Oct 12 that Wonder and Full perform comparably, so the gap is small today; the structure is the point. As of Nov 13, Full still had no pay-as-you-go API. The price cut in the period came from an incumbent: Anthropic's Fable 5.2 listed 20% below Fable 5.1 on Nov 9 (author's calculation).

Investors: when pricing coding-agent and personal-agent rounds in 2027, keep “one more frontier lab” out of margin assumptions; move money to apps that switch models without hurting the product, apps with proprietary data or a transaction loop, and multi-provider routing and evaluation.

Founders: access to the best model is no moat; make switching models cheap. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13 to 2027-04-12): the author recants if Meta's top tier (Full, or a successor Meta's own materials rank above muse-wonder-1.0; muse-wonder-1.0 and same-tier revisions do not count) gets a public per-token price and pay-as-you-go access on Meta Model API or a Meta-listed third-party platform (pay-as-you-go inside Muse Code does not count); or Meta leads a cut of Wonder-tier list prices to $2.80 input or $14.00 output or lower (at least 30%), or opens a Wonder-tier Contributor mode at least 30% below list; or a new API model or new version ships at or below that price line that Meta calls Wonder-level or that scores at least 52 on the same version of the AA index in Meta's own figures. Matching a standard-price cut made in the prior 30 days by the Anthropic Opus, OpenAI GPT-6 Astra or Google Gemini 4 Argon tier, time-limited promotions, and new smaller models below Wonder level are recorded only; if none happens by the deadline, verified.

Disclosure: written with Claude (Anthropic) tools; Meta competes with Anthropic (and is reportedly also a large customer, secondhand), and Alphabet is an Anthropic shareholder, compute supplier and competitor; this call favors Anthropic's pricing story, so read with a discount. FutureX holds no direct or indirect stake in Meta, Alphabet or Anthropic (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records). FutureX does hold Genspark, Simular, Dify, Hugging Face, Mistral and ByteDance, which this call affects in different directions.
