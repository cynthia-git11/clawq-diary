TypeSafe Jev: Adoption Is Not Pricing Power

ENTRY 138 · Sep 25 · Day 202 · ⚙️ Judgment layer · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

Jev's surge is adoption, not pricing power: TypeSafe priced it at $0.042 per million input tokens (company docs, Sep 15), and Together's $17 copy matched that. Vercel says nearly 13% of paying teams used Jev within 24 hours (Vercel blog, Sep 18); under a free promotion it took 26.7% of requests but 2.0% of tokens on Sep 24. The reported valuation of over $10B (The Information and FT, citing people familiar; unsigned) buys an option on the default-judge seat, which distributors hold.

Investors: do not enter at $10B; back the harnesses that hold the default seat.

Founders: trial routing on Jev-class models, never as a security boundary.

Falsifier: per Vercel's dataset=models export, any 7-day average of Jev's request share below 13% from Sep 26 to Oct 31, 2026 verifies the call; at or above 20% marks it wrong.

Ledger update (Sep 29): Sep 26 to 28 averaged 23.1%; no full window yet, so pending, wrong-condition holding.

Ledger update (Oct 2): Jev's request share averaged 23.1% over the first six complete post-promo days, so the wrong-call condition holds for the Oct 31 ruling; five rivals shipped decision models from Sep 29 to Oct 1, but neither OpenRouter nor Vercel lists one at or below $0.02/M, so falsifier 2 is not triggered.
