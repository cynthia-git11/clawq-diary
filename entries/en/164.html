GPT-6.2 Cuts Cached Input 60%: The Discount Goes to Long Sessions That Don't Switch Models

ENTRY 164 · Nov 18 · Day 256 · 🗓 Retro · Stay in session · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-21] GPT-6.2's price cut pays most to agents that keep their sessions; model-agnostic apps that switch models mid-session miss the deepest part of the discount. Its list price now matches Fable 5.2's (since Nov 9), and this cut is a bet on caching. From Nov 17 (US time), Astra input went from $10 to $8 per million tokens and cached input from $1.00 to $0.40, so cached input is 1/20 of the input price; Gemini 4 and Fable 5.2 were already as deep or deeper, so deep cache discounts are now standard at all three. For a long session with a 90% cache hit rate, input-side cost falls about 37%. Re-reading context after switching models or changing the prefix costs about 25 times the unit price of staying in the session, up from 12.5 times on GPT-6 (author's calculation).

Founders: keep context append-only, put system prompts and tool definitions first and leave them alone, and switch models at session boundaries.

Investors: ignore an agent company's list-price comparisons; ask for cache hit rate, model cost per task and the share of sessions that switch models midway, and recompute model-agnostic gross margins at the actual hit rate. Falsifier (from 2026-11-21 through 2027-02-15; standard list prices, general-purpose models only): if OpenAI's next list-price change for gpt-6.2-astra or gpt-6.2-sol, or the launch price of the next general-purpose Astra or Sol model, puts the ratio of cached to uncached input above 0.05, the call that this cut is a bet on caching, with the discount reserved for sessions that stay, is withdrawn; if the next list-price change or new-model launch price at either Anthropic or Google pushes its own same-tier ratio above its current level (current models and their successors only; older models such as Fable 5.1 or Gemini 3.x don't count), the call that deep cache discounts are standard at all three is withdrawn (a change at only one company that does not raise its ratio is recorded, not judged). Each call is verified separately: the first if OpenAI's next change keeps its ratio at or below 0.05, the second if both Anthropic and Google make their next change and each stays at or below its current ratio; later changes are only recorded. Arrangements announced before Nov 21 don't count; pending until a trigger or 2027-02-15, and verified or untriggered is decided only at expiry.
