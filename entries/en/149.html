Cities Still Buy Plate Readers; Flock's Cross-Jurisdiction Premium Is Due for Repricing

ENTRY 149 · Oct 19 · Day 226 · 🗓 Retro: network premium · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Cities are still buying license-plate readers; what is shrinking is the cross-jurisdiction network, and the network premium in Flock's valuation is due to be repriced (an inference). Flock raised at $8.3B in April 2026 (company-stated) and covered more than 5,000 communities in August. In Apex, N.C., payers and users differ: the police chief told ABC11 (reported Oct 22) that about 99% of searches of Apex data come from other agencies, and that Apex no longer shares nationwide, only with North Carolina and four neighboring states (date of the change not disclosed). Some leavers exit outright (Alexandria announced on Oct 28); others go to incumbent Axon: LAPD did not renew Flock in July, on Oct 27 the city council fell one vote short of blocking an Axon pilot, and at least four more places were reported switching between Oct 22 and Nov 12.

Investors: within 6–18 months the valuation anchor for government AI hardware moves from communities covered to renewal rate (an inference); in diligence, ask first for the share of contracts whose sharing clause was deleted or narrowed.

Founders: keep data in the customer's jurisdiction by default, and turn sharing on partner by partner, revocably. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13, not counted as a prior call): if by 2027-06-30 Flock completes a round, company tender or sale at $8.3B or more, or any of the five places named in the footnote as switching to Axon re-signs a Flock plate-reader contract, or any of them stops using plate readers without switching vendor, the call is wrong; a pricing event at $7.5B or less with reported ARR of at least $500M (a multiple squeeze, not a revenue drop), and with none of the five returning to Flock or voting to drop plate readers, verifies it (marked 'ARR missing' if ARR is undisclosed); between $7.5B and $8.3B is recorded only, and the final verdict is neither falsified nor verified; final check 2027-07-31.
