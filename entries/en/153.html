Anthropic's S-1: A Year of Compute Bills Roughly Equals Annualized Revenue

ENTRY 153 · Oct 27 · Day 234 · 🗓 Retro · commitments · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] If you are pricing ANT, read the compute-commitment note first; the income statement says little. In the Oct 26 S-1, $72.4B of the $80.7B H1 net loss was fair-value changes on convertible notes and similar instruments that rose with the valuation: no cash out; the cost is dilution. The Nov 10 amendment No. 2 says about $70.7B of purchase commitments and leases fall due in the 12 months from Sep 30, 1.04x Q3 GAAP revenue ($16.96B) times four (author's calculation). The Google and Amazon portions must be paid even if unused and are non-cancelable; both also hold equity, so they sit on the contract layer and the equity layer, while new IPO shareholders hold only the junior equity layer. The next year is covered: Sep 30 cash plus short-term investments of $131.9B is 1.87x that bill (author's calculation).

Investors: for ANT, recompute commitments due within a year divided by GAAP quarterly revenue times four every quarter, and compress the revenue multiple while it stays above 1; in model-layer private rounds, ask for the same table first.

Founders: manage multi-model bargaining as gross margin; Replit reportedly moved most of its AI model spend from Anthropic to OpenAI (The Information, Oct 24, single source). Falsifier (from 2026-11-13, not counted as an ex-ante call; final check 2027-05-31, SEC EDGAR): R = commitments due in the next 12 months divided by single-quarter GAAP revenue times four, now about 1.04. If either of ANT's first two periodic reports after listing shows R at or below 0.83 (revenue times four at least about 1.2x the 12-month commitments), the call is withdrawn; both at 1.30 or above verifies it; otherwise pending, and still in between at the final check it is recorded as undecided. Company-defined Run-Rate Revenue is not used.

Disclosure: written with Claude (Anthropic) tools; Google, Amazon and Microsoft are Anthropic investors and compute suppliers, Broadcom supplies accelerators and leases equipment to Anthropic, OpenAI is a competitor, Replit is an API customer and a coding-product competitor. FutureX holds no direct or indirect stake in Anthropic, Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, Broadcom, OpenAI or Replit, and holds Mistral and ByteDance, which compete with Anthropic (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records).
