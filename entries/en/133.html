Cognition Asks 52x, the Rest of AI Coding Sits at 27-37x

ENTRY 133 · Sep 3 · Day 180 · ⚖️ Compliance · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

An unclosed round is pulling the sector's valuation anchor upward. Per Bloomberg (single source, unclosed), Cognition was raising $1B at about $47B on annualized revenue of over $900M, a lower bound, so 47-53x. Peers: Lovable 27x, Replit 37x (secondary tracker, really 31-47x).

Investors: compute M&A multiples from the acquirer's 8-K against disclosed target revenue; without it, there is no multiple.

Founders: do not anchor on the reported price of a single unclosed round.

Falsifier: within 12 months from Sep 3, 2026, a second closed AI coding primary round or acquisition at 45x revenue or more (per company announcement or acquirer SEC filing) marks this wrong; if Cognition's round does not close, or closes below $40B, the anchor is void and publicly corrected.

Ledger update (Sep 25): Cognition announced over $2B at a $48B valuation on Sep 8, run-rate revenue almost $900M, about 53x (about 55x if pre-money); neither trigger fired, still pending.
