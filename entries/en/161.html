After Jensen Huang, Nvidia's New Guarantees and Strategic Stakes Tighten First

ENTRY 161 · Nov 12 · Day 250 · 🗓 Retro · New guarantees · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] AI clouds that borrow against new Nvidia backstops will pay more for their next debt (an inference). After Nvidia announced Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov 12, the guarantees and equity it provides to customers and partners split in two: signed deals run as agreed, new ones tighten first (an inference). Interim CEO Colette Kress is still CFO, with a first-ever board chair, independent director Harvey C. Jones, above her; the board's Nov 8 investigation had announced no findings as of Nov 12; and Democrats plan closer oversight of Nvidia's circular financing (Bloomberg, Nov 6). The board said it expects customer commitments to continue without interruption, and NVDA closed down 4.3% on Nov 12 (Nasdaq, author's calculation). Per its 10-Q filed Aug 26 (company figures; guarantees include those signed in August): maximum guarantee exposure $108.5B, including the OpenAI lease guarantee covered in ENTRY 126; commitments to buy back cloud capacity from AI clouds $36B; equity investment commitments $25B.

Investors: count signed Nvidia guarantees at contract value, but treat Nvidia following on or backstopping again as a contingency, and re-price a portfolio company's next financing without a new guarantee; in diligence, separate supplier financing from real customer revenue.

Founders: if your compute provider depends on new Nvidia guarantees to get financed, sign a second provider now. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13): if Nvidia signs any single new equity investment or convertible in a customer or ecosystem partner, or any guarantee, cloud buyback commitment or capacity backstop, of $1B or more between 2026-11-13 and 2027-02-12 (including exercising the extra ~3.8 GW Ohio credit support; performance of commitments binding before Nov 13, acquisitions including Hugging Face, and supply prepayments don't count), the call is withdrawn. None by then, plus no unexplained net rise of more than $1B in the three commitment lines of the FY27 10-K versus Q3 (as of Oct 25), net of scheduled pay-downs and closings, verifies it, though only weakly (about a 24% chance of zero by luck, author's calculation); until then, pending. Deals reported only by media, with no filing or announcement by either party, are marked source missing. Final check 2027-03-15.

Disclosure: this diary is written with Claude (Anthropic) tools; Nvidia and AMD are both buyers in Anthropic's concurrent IPO private placement (about $10B and $2.5B per Anthropic's Nov 10 S-1/A), and OpenAI is an Anthropic competitor, so discount any reading that helps or hurts Anthropic. FutureX holds no direct or fund-level indirect stake in Nvidia, OpenAI, AMD, Apple, Microsoft, Arm, Megaspeed or CoreWeave, and no direct or indirect stake in Anthropic (confirmed by the author from FutureX's records). FutureX does hold Hugging Face, which Nvidia announced on Sep 3, 2026 it would acquire (pending closing), and Mistral, whose Sep 2026 round Nvidia joined (secondhand); this entry does not forecast the closing and pre-announces no action on any holding.
