Goodfire Says Neurons Are Readable: Audit Money Goes to Whoever Can Reach the Weights

ENTRY 148 · Oct 17 · Day 224 · 🗓 Retro · audit rights · Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital · 中文原文 · Chinese original

[Retrospective, written 2026-11-13] Companies selling agents to banks, insurers and hospitals should prepare for a new procurement bar: can the model's internal state be audited independently (an inference)? That audit has to touch model internals. With open weights the buyer can do it; a closed model can be audited only by the vendor and the evaluators it admits, so the buyer needs the vendor to open an interface. Whether Goodfire is right sets Goodfire's price, not this dividing line. On Oct 15 Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata wrote that in a preliminary audit of Qwen3-32B about 90% of neurons have one main job (company claim, called a working hypothesis). The Nov 10 companion paper changed both model and measure: its statistics cover only Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and it found 69% on a different measure (main mode at least 90% of ablation damage) (company claim); no figure for the trillion-parameter Kimi K3 was public by Nov 13. Activation-probe monitors that do not rely on that result were already in production on Chinese open-weight models on Oct 8, at about 1/50 the cost of an LLM judge on every turn (company claim).

Investors: Goodfire was valued at $1.25B in its Feb 5, 2026 Series B (company claim); value interpretability companies on signed deployment-monitoring contracts first, and pay next round only for what others have replicated.

Founders: make internal monitoring a model-selection criterion; on closed models, write a monitoring interface or vendor monitoring reports into the contract. Falsifier (from 2026-11-13, final check 2027-07-15): (1) if, by 2027-06-30, a team outside Goodfire (or Goodfire itself) reports fewer than half of neuron poles single-mode dominated on any non-Qwen LLM family, using the Nov 10 paper's definition, the audit in this call narrows to activation-level monitoring and interpretability companies are valued on activation-monitoring contracts only; outside teams reporting a majority on two or more non-Qwen families verifies it, and if both happen the narrower reading applies. (2) If no regulated bank, insurer, health provider or their regulator publicly lists internal-state auditability as a procurement or deployment condition between 2026-11-13 and 2027-06-30, the call that procurement will check independent auditability and the second-order chain are withdrawn; only a condition carried out by the buyer or an independent third party it appoints verifies it, and vendor self-attestation or vendor monitoring reports alone are recorded as partial, not counted; current value zero, checked at the end with fixed news searches and regulators' sites, pending until then.
