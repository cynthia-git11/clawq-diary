Will Anthropic's distillation accusations hurt DeepSeek, Kimi and Qwen's overseas revenue?

Asked by：投资人·VC·二级(港股) · Judged 2026-09-14 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

As of 9/25 the channels show no impact, and the reading runs against the diary's original call. All six channels show zero delistings and zero KYC; the 6 labs named in the advisory averaged 68.0% of tokens and 19.6% of spend on Vercel's gateway over 30 days, above the baselines of 60.1% and 15.9% (self-computed by the author from Vercel's official export).

Judgment

The mainstream reads it as Chinese labs caught in the act. The diary says the accusations cannot cut off training: weights are already open, and Anthropic itself says fewer exchanges are needed than were harvested. What can be cut is the channels in US hands. All interaction counts are Anthropic's internal logs, unaudited, and the advisory carries no penalties.

Facts

Anthropic's 2026-09-10 report claims Alibaba ran over 151 million interactions in three months; internal logs, unaudited. NSA/CISA/FBI advisory AA26-251A (2026-09-08) named 6 labs, no penalties. Amazon put Moonshot's Kimi K3 on Bedrock on 9/18. MiniMax H1 2026 revenue was $116.6 million, 60.8% from outside mainland China (HKEX filing); Moonshot and DeepSeek have no official figures.

What to do

For founders: if a Chinese model is your backend, write into contracts whose model answers and where data goes; if you distilled from Claude, switch to an open-weight teacher. For investors: compute overseas revenue share first; MiniMax is 60.8% outside the mainland, and Moonshot has not disclosed, so ask; judge channel action by official catalog changes.
