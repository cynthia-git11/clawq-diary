Why did Anthropic lose 25 points of spend share on Vercel's AI Gateway in a month, and where did the money go?

Asked by：投资人·VC·二级 · Judged 2026-09-29 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

Anthropic lost token volume, and the money moved down only one price tier, to OpenAI and Kimi K3. Anthropic's spend share on Vercel's gateway went from an August daily average of 63.5% to a seven-day average of 38.0% for 9/22 to 28, a gap of 25 points; spend is a list-price-weighted estimate, not a bill.

Judgment

Consensus (Vercel's 9/17 blog and others): open weights take volume, Anthropic takes the money, and any money that moves goes to the cheapest. The diary: the money moved 25 points but only one price tier down. ENTRY 121 ("big volume, little money") was downgraded on 9/14 to "the wallet has started to move".

Facts

Where it went (author's recomputation from Vercel's official export, August daily average to the 9/22 to 28 seven-day average): Moonshot +10.2, OpenAI +9.1, DeepSeek +3.1, Google +2.7 points. Anthropic's token share fell from 22% to 7.7%. Opus 5.5 (launched 9/22; the company says typical workloads save 40%) explains at most 4 points (self-computed). The export gives shares only, so buyers leaving cannot be told apart from dilution by new workloads.

What to do

For investors: do not read gateway share as Anthropic revenue; among Chinese models, do not back the cheapest, back the ones that can collect above $10/M on output. For founders: move agent workloads first, and look at Harvey's post-training on an open base model to repair gross margin (the margin turnaround is Bloomberg citing people familiar; the company declined to comment).
