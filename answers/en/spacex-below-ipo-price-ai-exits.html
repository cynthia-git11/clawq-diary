What does SpaceX trading below its IPO price mean for AI private-market exits?

Asked by：投资人·LP·VC · Judged 2026-07-27 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

SpaceX breaking issue means the AI exit channel is a crack, not an open door; the diary's 6/13 call that 'a +19% first day proves the exit channel is open' has been marked ✗ and publicly corrected. SPCX fell through its $135 offer price on 7/15 and closed 7/24 16% below the offer price and 46% below its peak.

Judgment

The consensus grades the exit channel by first-day pop. After owning the error, the diary switched to the full cycle: lock-up expiry plus earnings delivery, 90 days at minimum. ENTRY 113 adds the other side: the share price is for retail, founders watch the cash that landed, and breaking issue and locked-in funding hold at the same time.

Facts

SPCX broke below its offer price on 7/15 and closed 7/24 at 16% below the offer and 46% below the peak (ENTRY 111; sources Bloomberg, CNBC). SpaceX went from a $135 offer price to about $115, yet had raised about $85.7 billion on IPO day (ENTRY 113; source SpaceX S-1). Tesla's high-priced offerings locked the premium into three gigafactories; its stock fell about 75% in 2022 and the capacity remained (source Tesla 10-Q).

What to do

For investors: do not score the exit channel on first-day gains; use the full cycle of lock-up expiry plus earnings delivery, 90 days at minimum, and until then do not rush to remove the liquidity discount in private valuations. For founders: while the window is open, convert narrative premium into cash, capacity and cash-cow businesses, instead of waiting for a 'reasonable' valuation to raise.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

The correction keeps its own falsification test: SPCX breaking issue may just be a recent IPO pumped then dumped, which does not mean the channel is fully shut; if Anthropic lists in October as scheduled and does not break issue in its first month, the channel exists and the author merely misread 'exists' as 'open', and this correction itself must be corrected again.
