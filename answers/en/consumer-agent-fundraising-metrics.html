What metrics should a consumer agent startup raise on: do downloads still count?

Asked by：创始人·消费级 agent 创始人 · Judged 2026-09-25 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

Downloads are not enough: a consumer agent should raise on merchant integrations and checkout conversion. Meta Muse had about 2.5M US downloads and 557K DAU through 9/19, both third-party estimates (Sensor Tower, as cited by CNBC and BofA); Meta has published zero official numbers.

Judgment

Consensus (JPM, 9/24) treats the transaction fee as long-term monetization with no near-term focus. The diary splits here: the fee is the product itself, the valuation variables are merchant integrations and whether Amazon opens its door, so success depends on how many merchants connect, not on DAU.

Facts

Sensor Tower estimates Meta Muse at about 2.5M US downloads and 557K DAU through 9/19, a same-source DAU/download ratio of 22% (no original post; cited via CNBC and BofA). Appfigures, 9/19: $27K net in-app revenue in the first 11 days, counting App Store in-app purchases only; Muse subscriptions run through Stripe, so this figure cannot serve as a monetization metric. Amazon blocked Muse on 9/20 (Bloomberg, citing a spokesperson).

What to do

For founders: replace downloads in the deck with merchant integrations and checkout conversion, and connect payment rails first. For investors: in diligence on a consumer agent, ask how many merchants are connected and whose payment rail handles checkout; treat third-party download estimates as reference only.
