Selling AI agents to banks and insurers: what new procurement checks should I prepare for?

Asked by: Founder · Judged 2026-11-13 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

The diary infers that procurement will add two gates (both from retrospective entries written 2026-11-13): first, whether the model's internal state can be audited independently, which the buyer can do itself with open weights, while with a closed model the contract has to require a monitoring interface or reports; second, which machines the tool can reach, whether one person can trigger a transfer and whether access can be revoked in one step, which the buyer will review as hot-wallet exposure. The case behind the second gate is the Binance theft, with about $6.34 billion of assets impacted (company figure).

Judgment

The mainstream is arguing over whether Goodfire's claim that neurons are readable is right, and the security community's consensus on the Binance case is that the internal AI tool's monitoring and permissions were poorly controlled; the diary's increment is only on the buyer side and the procurement bar: an audit has to touch model internals, so whoever can reach the weights can audit; machines an AI tool can reach should be managed like hot-wallet keys. The diary claims only that AI tools widened what attackers could reach, not that they were necessary. Contrary reading: regulated buyers currently accept vendor-provided monitoring, so "procurement will check independent auditability" runs against that status quo and is an inference. The Binance side has a contrary reading too: large institutions may build their own (Binance said on 11/3 it had rebuilt the AI tool and its access controls), so the opening for third-party vendors is more likely at mid-sized institutions and agent sellers.

Facts

ENTRY 148: Goodfire says it was already running activation-probe monitors on Chinese open-weight models on 10/8, at about 1/50 the cost of LLM review of every turn (company claim); as of 11/13 the diary had found no independent replication of its neuron-level result on a non-Qwen model family, and no regulated institution listing internal auditing as a procurement condition. On the closed side, per Anthropic's S-1, Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, which it announced in September, was co-developed with more than 100 financial, healthcare and public-sector customers; in the diary's reading it looks at behaviour and logs without opening internal state. ENTRY 155: Binance's 11/3 report says attackers used one engineer's computer and an internal AI tool to take over two other wallet engineers' computers, found a container deployment token, and on 10/29 (UTC) used it to call the wallet signing service; the tool's name and permissions were not disclosed; a malicious EIP-7702 authorization cannot be revoked immediately and needs a separate transaction to clear (company claim).

What to do

For founders: make the ability to plug in internal monitoring a model-selection criterion, and on a closed model require a monitoring interface or reports in the contract; make no-single-person triggering and one-click revocation the default, and have a permissions list ready showing which machines the tool can reach and what it can sign. For investors: in diligence ask what a portfolio agent can sign and whether its access can be revoked in one step; price interpretability companies on deployment-monitoring contracts first, and pay only for what others have replicated.
