What revenue multiple is expensive for AI coding companies? Is Cognition at $47B overpriced?

Asked by：投资人·VC · Judged 2026-09-03 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

Expensive. Cognition is the only 50x-class reading in the sector; everything else in the same period sits at 27-37x. On 9/8 the company announced a $48 billion valuation with over $2 billion raised, without stating pre- or post-money, about 53x; the earlier price, reported by Bloomberg as the single source and not yet closed, was about $47 billion.

Judgment

The mainstream treats Cognition's price as the sector's new anchor. The diary holds that 52x is a single observation in the whole sector and that the round had not closed when it set the anchor; both 'annualized revenue' and 'run-rate' are unaudited, and Cognition has never disclosed GAAP revenue, gross margin or net retention.

Facts

Cursor: 29x at its 2025-11 Series D; the SpaceX 8-K records consideration of $60 billion in all stock, closed 2026-08-14, and the roughly 30x is an estimate on a conservative $2 billion revenue assumption. Lovable 27x (TechCrunch 2026-08-12 Series C, run-rate basis). Replit 37x (2026-03 Series D; revenue from secondary trackers, error about ±20%, so really a 31-47x range).

What to do

For founders: do not anchor on a single round's reported price; 52x is one observation in the whole sector. For investors: compute M&A multiples from the acquirer's 8-K consideration terms paired with disclosed target revenue; if the filing has no target revenue, treat the multiple as nonexistent.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

Within 12 months from 2026-09-03: if a second closed AI coding primary round or acquisition appears at a revenue multiple of 45x or above (per company announcement or the acquirer's SEC filing), '52x is an outlier' fails and this entry is marked ✗; if Cognition's round does not close, or closes below a $40 billion valuation, this entry's anchor is void and a public correction follows. Adjudication sources are company announcements and SEC filings, with the reading published at that time.
