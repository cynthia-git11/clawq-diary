Is TypeSafe's Jev worth $10 billion? Can $0.042 per million tokens make money?

Asked by：投资人·VC · Judged 2026-09-25 · Ledger updated 2026-10-02 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

Do not enter at the media-reported $10 billion offer valuation; $0.042 is a ceiling, and the diary has no cost data to say whether it makes money. Jev lists at $0.042 per million input tokens with free output; on Vercel on 9/24, during a free promotion, it had 26.7% of requests, 2.0% of tokens, and was absent from the spend top ten.

Judgment

The mainstream story reads Jev's breakout as pricing power. The diary reads it as adoption: the decision layer commoditized within a week, and the media-reported $10 billion offer buys an option on the harness's default-judge seat, which the distributor controls. Jev is invisible on the spend board because it was free.

Facts

TypeSafe docs (9/15): $0.042/M input, output free. Together's blog (9/23) claims it trained an imitation for $17. Vercel official export, 9/24: 26.7% of requests, 2.0% of tokens, during a free promotion. TypeSafe's own eval: Jev 67.8%, tied with Luna. The Information and FT, both citing people familiar: valuation above $10 billion, not signed, not announced by the company.

What to do

For founders: move classification, routing and gatekeeping onto Jev-class models as a trial, do not treat it as a security boundary, and build your own calibration tests. For investors: do not enter at the media-reported $10 billion offer valuation; if you invest, back the harnesses and eval tools that hold the default seat, and wait for TypeSafe to disclose non-token revenue.
