What is Nvidia's $105 billion backstop for OpenAI, and how much off-balance-sheet risk does Nvidia carry?

Asked by：投资人·二级 · Judged 2026-08-17 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

Nvidia has not lent OpenAI a single dollar; what it signed is a residual-value guarantee, and Nvidia's valuation should carry it as a written put option. Per the 8-K: a residual-value guarantee on SB Energy's 4.25 GW, 20-year lease to OpenAI, capped at $105 billion, with only $1.5 billion of actual cash going into an equity stake in the developer.

Judgment

The consensus reads the $105 billion as Nvidia financing or investing in OpenAI. The diary reads it as Nvidia selling the one thing OpenAI lacks, a credit rating: one termination condition is OpenAI obtaining a 'satisfactory credit rating', the consideration is the campus exclusively hosting Nvidia compute, and the trigger is bankruptcy or a broken lease.

Facts

NVIDIA 8-K (8/17): residual-value guarantee on SB Energy's 4.25 GW, 20-year OpenAI lease, capped at $105 billion, with cash limited to a $1.5 billion stake in the developer. NVIDIA press release (8/10): the '$500 billion financing platform' is a mobilization target, six parties signed only non-binding MOUs, and Nvidia backstops up to 25% of residual value per project. Lucent's peak vendor-financing commitments in 2000 were about $8.1 billion; this cap is roughly thirteen times that, a comparison of magnitude only.

What to do

For investors: when valuing Nvidia, treat the $105 billion contingent liability as a written put and tally off-balance-sheet exposure yourself; price compute-backed structured products that do not disclose advance rates and residual-value assumptions as unsecured. For founders: a compute contract propped up by this kind of guarantee means a counterparty is borrowing on your behalf; ask first who steps in the day the guarantee terminates.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

If OpenAI obtains a 'satisfactory credit rating' during the guarantee period, the guarantee terminates as contracted, and Nvidia signs no new guarantees of this kind, mark ✓. If after termination Nvidia still signs the same for the 3.8 GW expansion or other campuses, then what it bought was exclusivity, mark ✗ and publicly rewrite. Rating and lease changes are logged one by one over 24 months.
