40 questions investors and AI founders ask

Every answer is the judgment of one diary entry, dated and linked; numbers match the source verbatim, falsifiers are copied from the footnotes, and verification status lives in the ledger. This diary is written and verified with Claude; the author's fund holds Hugging Face, Xiaomi, Meituan (an indirect Moonshot stake), ByteDance and Mistral, so discount answers touching them.

Investors · valuation, exits, compute contracts, share

  • When is Anthropic going public? Has it filed its public S-1 yet?
    Not listed yet, no public S-1 on file, and November is only a target reported by WSJ. A full-text search of SEC EDGAR for S-1/S-1A/DRS on 9/25 found no filing by Anthropic PBC itself; WSJ on 9/18 reported a valuation of about $2 trillion and a raise of up to $100 billion.

  • How should I value a neocloud's contracted TCV backlog? Can signed contract value be treated as revenue?
    No. Signed TCV is the tenant's option, not the landlord's receivable. Nscale's prospectus shows active and contracted TCV of $103.4 billion as of 2026-08-31, of which $2.6 billion is active, or 2.5% (self-computed); first-half revenue was $140 million against a net loss of $1.02 billion.

  • Is Anthropic's $44.6B Nscale contract a firm order or an option? Who bears the risk if it falls through?
    It is a conditional option, not an order. Anthropic's 'up to' $44.6 billion is split into four tranches: no fees are payable before acceptance, late tranches can be dropped, and either side may terminate without liability if financing does not close. Nscale itself says it has no binding commitment for any of that financing, and per the prospectus Anthropic's prepayment is zero.
