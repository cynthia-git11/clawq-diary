Will the Sony/Warner copyright lawsuit derail Anthropic's IPO, and how much could it cost?

Asked by：投资人·二级·家办 · Judged 2026-08-31 · Ledger updated 2026-10-02 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

The damages are computable and barely move the valuation; what can move the IPO is the offering window, a destruction order and the founders' personal liability. Taking the books case as reference ($1.5 billion settlement, 482460 works, $3109 per work), the same ratio applied to the music side comes to about two ten-thousandths of Anthropic's $965 billion post-money valuation.

Judgment

The mainstream sizes exposure from the damages figure. The diary starts from the offering calendar: the public S-1 must be disclosed at least 15 days before the roadshow, and an unquantifiable contingent liability inside that countdown moves the offering window and the price range. As of 9/25 the public S-1 had not been filed, and the publicly stated reason for the delay is third-quarter financials. The diary's own note says the books case and the music case are not comparable.

Facts

On 2026-08-28, 35 Sony and Warner publishing entities sued Anthropic, naming its two founders as individual defendants. Bartz books case: $1.5 billion settlement, 482460 works, $3109 per work, only 2.07% of the statutory maximum; $3109 is gross and is a price negotiated after liability was established, while liability in this case is not yet established. WSJ on 9/18 (as relayed by Investing.com) reported the listing target moved to November, with people familiar saying it was unrelated to the litigation.

What to do

For investors: watch the order of the public S-1 filing date versus the settlement date, and check whether the risk factors section lists this litigation; the destruction order and founders' personal liability are not priced per work, so estimate them separately. For founders: proof of training-data procurement is now part of the fundraising materials, not a legal-department matter.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

Within 12 months from 2026-08-31, if a second court-approved public settlement appears with a per-work price within one order of magnitude of $3109, 'damages do not move valuation' holds and is upgraded to verified; if Anthropic delays or withdraws its offering because of this litigation, the main judgment is marked ✓; if the public S-1 is filed on schedule and the offering is unaffected, this entry is downgraded.
