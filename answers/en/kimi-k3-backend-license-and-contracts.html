Building on Kimi K3 as my backend: what has to go into my license review and customer contracts?

Asked by：创始人·用国产开源模型的 SaaS / 出海创业者 · Judged 2026-09-14 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

The K3 license is not MIT; it sets gates by revenue line. Running K3 as MaaS with revenue above USD 20 million over 12 consecutive months requires a separate agreement with Moonshot AI first; a product above 100 million monthly active users or USD 20 million in monthly revenue must display "Kimi K3" in its interface (the entry listed no source line; not independently recovered).

Judgment

The mainstream reading is that open source means cheap and free to use. The diary's call is that "open source" released the weights and kept the pricing power, and the mechanism sits in the license: whoever reaches scale on the weights comes back to pay the toll. Customer contracts carry a second layer, the data-routing question raised by the distillation allegations, which are unproven.

Facts

Moonshot AI released the K3 weights in July 2026; 11 days before that, the official API output price went from ¥27 to ¥100 per million tokens, about 3.7 times (the entry listed no source line; not independently recovered). The weights are about 1.56TB, so self-hosting needs a multi-node cluster. Anthropic's September report alleges Moonshot silently forwarded user requests to Claude; all of it rests on Anthropic's internal logs, unaudited. Amazon listed Kimi K3 on Bedrock on 9/18.

What to do

For founders: treat USD 20 million in revenue as the license renegotiation point and open talks a year ahead, before the trigger; state in customer contracts who the replies come from and where the data goes. For investors: ask one diligence question, how much of this company's value disappears if the base model is swapped.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

If within one year the United States puts restrictions on the use of Chinese open weights into effect, and large companies broadly drop them, then "open weights are a business" is downgraded to geopolitical hostage and this call is withdrawn; the adjudicating sources are a formal executive order and public statements of abandonment by large companies.
