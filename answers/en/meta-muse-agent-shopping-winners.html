Will Meta's Muse succeed, and who actually benefits from agent commerce?

Asked by：投资人·二级 · Judged 2026-09-25 · 张倩 Cynthia Zhang · FutureX Capital

The diary does not conclude that Muse will succeed: its outcome turns on how many merchants agree to connect, not on DAU, and the beneficiaries sit at the Stripe, Shopify and PayPal layer rather than on the chat-app charts. Third-party estimates put US downloads at about 2.5 million through 9/19 and DAU at 557,000, with zero official numbers from Meta.

Judgment

Consensus (JPM, 9/24): monetization via take-rate is long-dated, with no near-term focus on monetization. The diary argues the fee is the product itself, that the valuation variables are merchant count and whether Amazon opens up, and that Amazon, the largest door, is lawfully shut.

Facts

Meta's official post: Muse launched in the US on 9/8 with checkout via Link by Stripe. Amazon began blocking on 9/20 (Bloomberg 9/21, citing a spokesperson). Shop Pay, Walmart and PayPal integrations are announced only. Sensor Tower, as relayed by CNBC/BofA with no original post: about 2.5 million US downloads through 9/19 and DAU of 557,000. Meta's FY26 total expense guidance is $165.0B to $169.0B.

What to do

For founders: do not raise on downloads for a consumer agent; raise on merchant connections and checkout conversion, and connect payment rails first. For investors: for the Muse option, first watch whether Meta raises its full-year expense guidance, and look for beneficiaries at the payments and merchant layer; this is the diary's judgment, not a rating or advice.

Falsifier (verbatim from the diary footnote)

Three lines, ruled separately. (1) "The largest door is lawfully shut": from 2026-09-25 to 2027-03-31, if Amazon opens ordering to Muse through an official statement or a joint announcement with Meta (ruling sources: Amazon newsroom, Meta official posts), mark ✗; if at expiry Amazon still blocks and there is no joint announcement, mark verified. (2) "A checkout-page business": by 2026-12-31, if Meta discloses Muse transaction volume or fee revenue in an earnings release, at Connect or in an official post, mark verified; if Meta discloses that Muse revenue is mainly subscriptions (subscription revenue above transaction-fee revenue), mark ✗; if neither, mark ⏳. (3) The reading line for "downloads are not worth much" (ruling source: Sensor Tower US DAU as relayed by CNBC/BofA/Benzinga, starting value 557,000 through 9/19; if no relay, Apptopia US mobile DAU via TechCrunch as backup, starting value 642,000, thresholds scaled proportionally; if neither, mark ruling source missing): by 2026-12-31, if DAU ≥1 million and DAU/downloads ≥30%, this line is withdrawn; if DAU ≤400,000, or Meta raises FY26 expense guidance and names Muse inference cost, this line is strengthened; in between, mark ⏳.
