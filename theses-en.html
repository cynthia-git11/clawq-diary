Method

Falsifiers. Each call is logged with the date it was written. 64 of the 92 cards carry an explicit falsifier line (证伪口) stating what would prove the call wrong; 20 of the other 28 carry a verification-signal (验收) or rollback (回滚) line instead.

Status changes. Status is the badge on the Chinese card. Changes are recorded on the card and added, never overwritten: 9 of the 18 resolved calls carry a dated reconciliation note (「YYYY-MM-DD 对账」); the other 9 were settled in the card's own text, which cites the entry or event that confirmed or overturned the call (3 of them through a separate correction entry).

Corrections are additions. A wrong call is not deleted: the correction is added to the card next to the original text and, where there is one, published as a separate correction entry. 8 of 92 calls are publicly corrected; their dates are listed above.

Git history. The repository is public. Each call links to the commit in which its entry first appeared in index.html (dates in Beijing time, UTC+8); 92 of 92 calls have that link (a new call is linked once its commit is on the public main branch). The wording at that commit may differ from today's page, and every later edit is in the same history.

Retrospective entries are counted separately. 22 entries were written after the event they discuss and are marked Retrospective with both dates. Of the 18 resolved calls, 18 were written before the outcome and 0 are retrospective.

English text. The English line on each call is the title of the English edition of its source entry, not a translation of the Chinese ledger claim. 8 calls have no English edition and are shown in Chinese.
